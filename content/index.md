---
title: The Annals of the Dragonslayers of Turos Tem
---
I, [[Hikarius Iudexius]], put my quill to parchment and inscribe here the tales of the group of adventurers known to history first as the Dragonslayers of Turos Tem. Though the slaying of the dragon Idimmu was the first of their exploits, it was far from their last; but such tales must come in their proper time. 

Until their tale is fully told, this record shall be a reference work for all their equipment, members, and deeds; those of which I see fit to write, of course. Of some matters I may say much, other matters may be left obscure. The presence of a matter in these annals does not imply anything more than absence does, save that I was able to recall and record it for posterity. 

While my hand alone started this work, I hope that in time it will include thoughts and words from the members of the Dragonslayers themselves. Such a work could hardly be considered complete without the perspectives of those who lived through those eventful days. 

It is my hope that this work serves you well, dear reader. May the reading of it bring you as much joy as the writing did me. 

~ [[Hikarius Iudexius]], Judge
