### Guests
Presided by **Councilor Arjenus Ionicus Drakomir.** 
Attended by wife Tara Dradanna and children Malynurio (22) and Adara (20). (Plus guards)
- Apologies are made for Tavis, he is resting and the house physician said the excitement would damage his health. 
Introductions, gratitude, small talk
- (Tara) "I'm pleasantly surprised, all I ever hear of 'adventurers' is how shabby and uncultured they are. But not only have you saved my son, I hear your party captured some fort across the Krysivor? You have surpassed my expectations."
- (Arjenus) "Indeed, darling. It just goes to show you that even the lowborn can achieve great things when granted station."
Malynurio and Adara are inquisitive about adventuring, asking/prying for information about their adventures. Adara has been seated by Willamar, and acts flirtatiously. 

Otherwise, the dinner passes with polite conversation. As the dishes are being removed, Arjenus invites the party to view his garden and discuss their reward. 

They are given a reward of 1000gp, and Arjenus asks to hear their version of events.
- (Think Tarkin) "I have only heard it in fragments, and through secondhand sources. I would very much like to hear it directly from my son's benefactors."
- After hearing "I am loathe to discuss family issues with outsiders, but I was told that my son had left the city in the company of a woman of... less than stellar repute. Did you find ought of her?"
- "I would consider it a personal favor if there *continued* to be naught heard of her. My son will overcome his childish infatuation with that Ambador vixen in due time, but her reappearance would make that process... difficult."
- "So long as his recovery - and her absence - continues, House Drakomir is willing to advocate on your behalf in the troublesome bureaucratic matters that come with establishing a new domain. You may also find it interesting to know that House Drakomir oversees taxation both here in Cyfaraun and throughout the Borderlands. I know adventurers tend to find that sort of thing vexing. Wouldn't it be nice to have no need to concern yourself with it any further?" 
- "House Drakomir is, may I humbly say, on the rise in the Borderlands."
