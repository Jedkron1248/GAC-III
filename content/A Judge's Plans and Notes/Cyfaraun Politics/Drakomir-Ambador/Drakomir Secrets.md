They had knowledge of Tavis and Zeodare sneaking out. They were following at a distance. The doppelganger sprung its trap and captured only Tavis, leaving behind Zeodare. 

Drakomir men captured Zeodare, and Arjenus knows. He had planned to use her as blackmail or something, suspecting Ambador had something to do with Tavis disappearing. When Ambador seemed just as clueless, and Tavis shows up talking about ambushes and doppelgangers, he realizes that he is holding a powerful card, but he can't be the one playing it. It's too obvious.

So, he hands her off to the Sand and Bones through several proxies, paying much and promising more. S&B demands Ambador step down from the council and retire to his lands, and after a month, Zeodare would be returned to him (more or less) unharmed. Otherwise, each day, a piece of her will be delivered to a different Ambador subsidiary. 

Zeodare is in a gamber's row townhouse connected to 4 other units, she's in number 5 on the right.
